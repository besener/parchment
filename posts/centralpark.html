Central Park

May 12, 2026

Over the weekend, I took a lovely walk in Central Park. The weather was around 73°F (23°C), so it felt like the perfect spring day to be outside among the trees.

I was in New York City to visit the Department of Islamic Art at the Metropolitan Museum of Art. I examined several metalware with the supervising object technician. Spending time in the object study room and meeting the staff at the MET was a real highlight.
